Welcome to the Workshoppe. This is where I write up what I make: the finished pieces, the failed attempts, and the jigs and tricks I picked up along the way.

I'm a maker. Most of my time at the bench goes to **woodworking**, and I'm now moving into **metalworking**, which is the "steel" half of the name. I also spend a lot of time on **3D printing** and **CAD**, because a good model on the screen makes the real cut cleaner, and a printed jig often saves a board.

The shop is a detached garage. It isn't fancy, and that's part of the point: you can make good things without a perfect space.

## What you'll find here

- Build write-ups, with photos of the process and not only the result.
- Multi-part **series** for bigger builds, so you can follow a project from start to finish.
- Downloadable files, such as cut lists and CAD or STL files, where they're useful.
