# Wood and Steel Workshop

A public, single-author maker blog at woodandsteelworkshoppe.com. It covers woodworking, metalworking, 3D printing, CAD and related making.

## Language

**Post**:
A dated article by the author. The only kind of content on the site.
_Avoid_: Entry, article, project, page

**Category**:
The one required subject area of a Post: woodworking, metalworking, 3D-printing, cad, or other.
_Avoid_: Topic, section

**Tag**:
A free-form label on a Post for finer-grained subjects such as a tool, material or technique.
_Avoid_: Keyword, label

**Series**:
An ordered group of Posts that together document one multi-part build, with navigation between them.
_Avoid_: Collection, build log, project

**Attachment**:
A file a reader can download from a Post, such as an STL, STEP file, cut list or PDF.
_Avoid_: Download, asset, resource

**Model**:
An interactive 3D view of an Attachment embedded in a Post.
_Avoid_: Viewer, 3D embed

**Draft**:
A Post that is not yet published and is not visible to readers.
_Avoid_: Unpublished, WIP
